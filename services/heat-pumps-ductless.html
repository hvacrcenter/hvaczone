Heat Pumps & Ductless Mini-Splits in Newark, NJ

Ducted heat pumps and ductless mini-splits installed, repaired and maintained in Newark, the towns that border it, and all of Essex County. A good fit for homes without ductwork, additions, and rooms the main system can’t keep comfortable.

Call (973) 687-6386 How pricing works

What we handle

  • Ductless mini-split installation: single-zone and multi-zone
  • Ducted heat pump replacements, including cold-climate models
  • Dual-fuel setups that pair a heat pump with an existing gas furnace
  • Thermostat and lockout settings so backup heat doesn’t run more than it should
  • Repair and maintenance: filters, coils, drains, refrigerant and controls

Signs it’s time to call

  • Rooms that are always too hot or too cold compared with the rest of the house
  • A home heated by a boiler with no ductwork for air conditioning
  • An addition, attic or basement room the main system doesn’t reach
  • Backup or emergency heat running often in moderate weather
  • Interest in cutting gas use or adding efficient cooling

Repair or replace?

Heat pumps work well in North Jersey winters when they are sized and set up correctly. The details that matter most are sizing to the heating load, the backup heat plan, and controls. We explain the trade-offs for your home before you decide.

How pricing works

  • New systems and replacements: free on-site estimate. We measure the space and look at the existing setup before quoting.
  • System not working right: a diagnostic visit has a fee, and the entire fee is applied toward the repair.
  • Larger jobs: financing is available, including for emergency system replacements.
  • Warranty: a labor warranty is standard on every replacement and new installation. Manufacturer equipment warranties carry their own terms, and we help you register the equipment.
