What we handle

  • Troubleshooting units that run but don’t cool, short-cycle, freeze up, leak water, or won’t start
  • Electrical repairs: capacitors, contactors, fan motors, controls and wiring
  • Refrigerant leak search and repair, done to EPA Section 608 rules
  • Full system replacements, sized for your space instead of matched to the old unit’s tonnage
  • Seasonal maintenance: coil cleaning, drain lines, electrical checks and airflow

Signs it’s time to call

  • Warm air from the vents, or the house never reaches the set temperature on a normal summer day
  • Ice on the copper line or the indoor coil
  • Water around the indoor unit or a full condensate pan
  • Loud buzzing, rattling, or the outdoor fan not spinning
  • Energy bills climbing year over year with the same use

Repair or replace?

Whether to repair or replace depends on the age and condition of the system, the refrigerant it uses, how often it has failed, and the cost of the repair compared with a new system. Systems that run on R-22, or need a major part like a compressor on an older unit, are often better candidates for replacement. We show you what we found and let you decide.

How pricing works

  • New systems and replacements: free on-site estimate. We measure the space and look at the existing setup before quoting.
  • System not working right: a diagnostic visit has a fee, and the entire fee is applied toward the repair.
  • Larger jobs: financing is available, including for emergency system replacements.
  • Warranty: a labor warranty is standard on every replacement and new installation. Manufacturer equipment warranties carry their own terms, and we help you register the equipment.
