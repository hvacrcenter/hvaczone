Industry ownership

Meet the brands

The HVAC industry has consolidated into a handful of global parents, so several “competing” labels are actually built by the same company. Here’s who owns whom today — and what each brand is positioned to offer. We stay neutral: knowing the family tree helps you compare value, not pick a logo.

Daikin Industries Japan Official site →
The world’s largest air-conditioning manufacturer.
DaikinPremium full line — high-efficiency furnaces, ACs and heat pumps, plus the industry’s widest ductless and VRF range.
GoodmanValue-oriented, builder-friendly equipment sold through distribution. Daikin’s high-volume brand.
AmanaValue tier sharing Goodman’s platform, set apart by stronger warranties — including a lifetime unit-replacement compressor warranty.
Carrier Global USA Official site →
Pioneered modern air conditioning; broad residential and commercial portfolio.
CarrierFlagship premium brand; full residential and commercial line.
BryantCarrier’s dealer-channel sister brand — near-identical equipment under a different nameplate.
PayneBuilder / entry-level tier of the Carrier family.
ICP brands: Heil, Tempstar, Comfortmaker, Arcoaire, Day & Night, KeepriteValue and contractor tiers sharing common platforms (International Comfort Products).
Viessmann Climate Solutions Acquired 2024European heat-pump and heating leader added to Carrier’s portfolio.
Trane Technologies USA / Ireland Official site →
Global climate-innovation company spanning residential and commercial.
TranePremium brand with deep commercial heritage and high-efficiency residential equipment.
American Standard Heating & Air ConditioningTrane’s consumer sister brand — comparable engineering under a different name.
OxboxContractor-direct, online-first value brand.
RunTruBudget / builder tier.
Thermo PrideRugged, long-life oil-fired furnaces.
Rheem Manufacturing USA / Paloma (Japan) Official site →
Air and water-heating specialist; owned by Paloma Industries of Japan.
RheemFull residential and commercial line sold through retail and contractor channels.
RuudContractor-channel sister brand — same equipment as Rheem under a different label.
Nortek / Nordyne family Acquired Oct 2024Frigidaire HVAC, Maytag HVAC, Miller, Gibson and Intertherm — value, retail and manufactured-housing tiers; plus Broan ventilation.
Fujitsu General Acquired 2025Ductless mini-split brand added to the Paloma/Rheem group.
Value tiers: Richmond, Sure Comfort, WeatherKingEntry-level and private-label water-heating and HVAC lines.
Bosch Home Comfort Group Germany New owner 2025 Official site →
Acquired Johnson Controls’ residential & light-commercial HVAC business in August 2025 — a major ownership shift.
YorkMid-tier dealer brand (formerly Johnson Controls).
ColemanManufactured-housing and residential value brand (formerly Johnson Controls).
LuxaireYork’s sister brand (formerly Johnson Controls).
ChampionValue tier (formerly Johnson Controls).
JCI–Hitachi Air Conditioning JVThe former Johnson Controls–Hitachi joint venture moved to Bosch with the deal.
Lennox International USA Official site →
Premium residential and commercial; dealer-only distribution.
LennoxFlagship premium brand known for high-efficiency, smart-home-ready equipment.
Armstrong AirLennox engineering at a lower price point — built by Allied Air Enterprises.
Allied Air Enterprises: AirEase, Concord, Ducane, Allied Commercial, MagicPakValue and specialty tiers under the Lennox-owned Allied umbrella.
Mitsubishi Electric Japan Official site →
Global leader in ductless and variable-refrigerant-flow systems.
Mitsubishi Electric (Mr. Slim, City Multi, M-/P-Series)Premium ductless mini-splits and VRF; strong cold-climate heat-pump performance.
Samsung South Korea Official site →
Tech-forward HVAC within the Samsung group.
Samsung HVACWindFree ductless and Digital VRF systems with smart-home integration.
LG Electronics South Korea Official site →
Design- and connectivity-focused HVAC.
LG HVACArt Cool and Multi F ductless, Multi V VRF — strong on design and smart controls.
Gree Electric Appliances China Official site →
One of the world’s largest air-conditioner manufacturers; a major OEM for other labels.
GreeHigh-volume residential and light-commercial full line.
TOSOTGree’s lifestyle sub-brand for comfort and appliances.
KINGHOMEGree sub-brand.
Midea Group China Official site →
One of the world’s largest appliance and HVAC manufacturers. Midea is a major OEM — it builds equipment sold under many other labels, not just its own name.
Cooper & HunterValue-oriented ductless brand (Ukraine-founded, distributed in the US by ComfortSide). Its mini-splits are rebadged Midea units — Midea is the manufacturer, not the brand’s corporate owner. Some models are sourced from Gree.
Also OEM-built on Midea platformsCarrier and Bryant ductless systems, the Bosch Climate 5000 series, MRCOOL and Durastar are widely reported to use Midea equipment.
TCL Technology China Growing fast Official site →
Consumer-electronics giant that has become one of the world’s largest air-conditioner exporters — roughly 20 million AC units a year across 160+ countries.
TCL Air Conditioner (T-Pro, FreshIN, BreezeIN, SaveIN AI)Ductless mini-splits, window units, portables and light-commercial systems; value-to-mid positioning with smart-home and AI energy-saving features. Sold via retail (e.g., Home Depot) and as OEM/ODM for other labels.
