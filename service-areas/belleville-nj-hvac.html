What that means for heating and cooling

  • Mostly older housing. About 61% of homes in Belleville were built before 1960, and 32% before 1940. Older homes often have hot-water or steam boilers with radiators, original chimneys and little or no ductwork. That makes boiler service, venting checks and ductless cooling common requests.
  • Mostly gas heat. About 73% of households heat with utility gas, 14% with electricity and 5% with fuel oil. With that much gas heat, annual combustion and carbon monoxide safety checks matter.
  • Some oil heat remains. About 5% of households still heat with fuel oil, so oil-to-gas or oil-to-heat-pump conversions come up.
  • Lots of multi-family buildings. 51% of homes are in buildings with two or more units (28% in two- to four-unit buildings). Units often have their own equipment, so access, shared venting and coordinating with owners are part of the job.
  • Most households own. About 44% of households rent and 56% own. Homeowners are usually the ones deciding on repair versus replacement, and we give you the information to make that call.

How pricing works

  • New systems and replacements: free on-site estimate. We measure the space and look at the existing setup before quoting.
  • System not working right: a diagnostic visit has a fee, and the entire fee is applied toward the repair.
  • Larger jobs: financing is available, including for emergency system replacements.
  • Warranty: a labor warranty is standard on every replacement and new installation. Manufacturer equipment warranties carry their own terms, and we help you register the equipment.
