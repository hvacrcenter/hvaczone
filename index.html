How pricing works

Estimates, diagnostics & what to expect

The first question most people ask is “How much?” — and that’s normal. Every job is different, so we need to see the job to price it accurately.

Free estimates for new systems & replacements

Pricing a new system or replacement means evaluating the space and the existing setup — measurements and a conversation about what you need. There is no charge for this estimate.

Diagnostic fee for system problems

When a system isn’t working right, troubleshooting means putting tools to work to find the cause. That diagnostic visit has a fee — and the entire fee is applied toward the repair.

Financing for larger jobs

Financing is available for bigger projects, including emergency system replacements. Call to discuss the options available for your job.

Warranty on installations & replacements

A labor warranty is standard with every system replacement and new installation. Manufacturer equipment warranties come with their own terms, and we help you with registration.

Why licensed contractors price the way they do: a licensed and insured contractor carries licensing, insurance, training, tools, and vehicles so the work is done to code and you are protected if something goes wrong. That is part of what you are paying for.

Indoor air quality

IAQ products we install & service

Clean air is half of comfort. These whole-home systems integrate with your HVAC equipment to filter, ventilate, and balance humidity — far beyond what a standard 1-inch filter can do. We specify, install, and service them all, with no single-brand bias.

Media air cleaners

High-MERV (MERV 13–16) media filtration built into your ductwork — captures dust, pollen, pet dander, and fine particles with minimal airflow loss.

UV-C germicidal lights

Ultraviolet light in the airstream and at the coil to reduce bacteria, viruses, and mold — and keep the evaporator coil clean for better efficiency.

Whole-home humidifiers

Bypass, fan-powered, and steam humidifiers that hold a healthy 30–50% relative humidity in winter — protecting sinuses, woodwork, and electronics.

Whole-home dehumidifiers

Tackle damp basements and humid summers, cut mold and dust-mite growth, and let you set the thermostat a few degrees higher while staying comfortable.

Ventilation (ERV / HRV)

Energy and heat recovery ventilators bring in fresh, filtered outdoor air and exhaust stale air while recovering the heating or cooling you already paid for.

Air purifiers & VOC control

Whole-home purifiers with carbon and photocatalytic stages that neutralize odors, smoke, and volatile organic compounds from cooking, cleaning, and furnishings.

Already monitoring your air? See our explainer on IAQ monitors & sensors. Guidance grounded in the U.S. EPA’s Indoor Air Quality resources. Not sure what you need? Request a consultation and we’ll assess your home or building.
